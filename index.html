Solo mining odds are lottery-style. A 10% chance does not mean you are due. You can hit early, late, or not at all.
chance = 1 - (1 - your_hashrate / network_hashrate) ^ expected_network_blocks
expected_network_blocks = rental_hours × 60 / block_interval_minutes
expected_blocks = chance_per_block × expected_network_blocks
estimated_hashrate = difficulty × 2^32 / block_time_seconds
